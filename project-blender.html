Case study

Ramen Noodle Cart

Role: 3D Artist & Designer | Tool: Blender

Project overview

This project was about creating a small but memorable world: a handcrafted ramen noodle cart set in a cozy street environment. I wanted the scene to feel warm, lived-in, and full of character, like a place where people would stop for a quick bowl of noodles after a long day.

Design direction

I designed the cart around a clear visual story: a compact, mobile food setup with simple surfaces, rich wood tones, glowing warm lighting, and a strong sense of atmosphere. The materials were intentionally stylized so the whole scene felt handcrafted instead of overly realistic. Every object, from the counter to the signage, was chosen to support the same cozy food-stall mood.

Creative challenge

The hardest part was making the cart feel believable without making the scene too busy. I focused on proportions, silhouette, and color balance so the final render would read clearly at a glance. I also paid close attention to the lighting, because the warm glow is what gives the entire composition its personality and makes the ramen cart feel alive.

Why this project matters to me

What I love most about this piece is how it blends storytelling, visual design, and 3D craft. It showed me that a small environment can still carry a strong identity when the mood, materials, and composition all work together. The ramen cart became a perfect example of how I like to design: focused, tactile, and full of atmosphere.

Project snapshot

  • Hand-crafted ramen cart scene in Blender
  • Used stylized materials to create a realistic-but-approachable feel
