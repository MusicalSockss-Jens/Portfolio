Case study

Ramen Noodle Cart

Role: 3D Artist & Designer | Tool: Blender

Project overview

This project was an exam piece for 3D Design I, focussed on creating a lowpoly ramen noodle cart in Blender.

Creative challenge

The hardest part was setting myself to it. And making the models look lived in whilst staying low poly, that's where good texturing came in, I even found a way to make the rust more matte than the rest of the metal. I didn't use tutorials either.

Design direction

The cart was designed how it is because I wanted to experiment with texturing in Procreate on my iPad, which allowed me to create unique surface details and materials and baked my textures more easily.

Why this project matters to me

It was a retake because my first projects was bad. N-gons, bad topology, bad unwrap, everything. This second try I threw myself into it, made my own textures in procreate, and didn't just pass, but i was proud of myself.

Project snapshot

  • Hand-crafted ramen cart scene in Blender
  • Used stylized materials to create a realistic-but-approachable feel
